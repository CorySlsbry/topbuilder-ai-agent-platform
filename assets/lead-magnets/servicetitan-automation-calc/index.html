The Playbook

10 Highest-ROI ServiceTitan Automations

Each automation below integrates with your existing ServiceTitan setup. No rip-and-replace.

01
Pricebook Sync

Connects to supplier pricing feeds and auto-updates your ServiceTitan pricebook to maintain target margins. When a supplier raises material costs, the system recalculates your flat-rate pricing, flags items where margins have eroded below threshold, and queues price changes for manager approval before pushing to techs in the field.

3-6 hrs/week saved Medium
ServiceTitan integration: Pricebook API for read/write, Custom Fields for margin tracking. Requires supplier price feed access (CSV upload or API).
02
Job Costing Automation

Pulls completed job data including technician time-on-site, materials used, and equipment charges. Cross-references against estimates to calculate actual job margin in real-time. Flags any job falling below your target gross profit percentage. Weekly digest shows margin trends by job type, technician, and department so you spot problems before they compound.

5-8 hrs/week saved Hard
ServiceTitan integration: Jobs API, Timesheet data, and Inventory records. Requires consistent material tracking on every job for accurate costing.
03
A/R Collections Sequence

Monitors aging invoices and triggers a multi-channel collection sequence. Day 7: friendly email with invoice attached and a one-click payment link. Day 14: SMS reminder. Day 21: call task assigned to your office manager with a pre-written script and account summary. Day 30: escalation alert to the owner. Adjusts tone and urgency based on customer payment history.

4-8 hrs/week saved Easy
ServiceTitan integration: Invoicing API and Customer Communications module. Connects to your email/SMS provider and payment gateway for automated links.
04
Dispatch Optimization

A skill-based routing engine that matches incoming jobs to the right technician based on certifications, proximity, drive time, current workload, and close rate. Minimizes windshield time by clustering jobs geographically. Factors in equipment requirements and customer history so your best closers land on the highest-value calls.

5-10 hrs/week saved Hard
ServiceTitan integration: Dispatch Board API, Technician Profiles, and GPS/routing data. Requires up-to-date tech skill tags and equipment assignments.
05
Estimate Follow-Up

Tracks every unsold estimate and triggers personalized follow-up. Day 1: thank-you email with estimate summary. Day 3: educational content about the proposed work. Day 7: personal check-in call task for your sales team. Day 14: modified offer or financing option. Reports close rates by follow-up stage so you know exactly where deals are falling out.

3-5 hrs/week saved Easy
ServiceTitan integration: Estimates API and Customer Portal. Connects to your email platform and CRM for sequence management.
06
Payroll Prep

Extracts technician hours from ServiceTitan timesheets, cross-references with dispatched job times, and identifies discrepancies between clocked hours and time on site. Calculates overtime, commission, and spiff payments automatically. Generates payroll-ready exports formatted for your provider, flagging entries that need manual review before submission.

4-8 hrs/week saved Medium
ServiceTitan integration: Timesheet API, Payroll Export, and Commission structures. Requires defined commission/spiff rules and payroll provider import format specs.
07
Customer Reactivation

Scans your customer database for accounts with no service activity in 12 or more months. Segments by last service type, equipment age, and lifetime value. Triggers targeted reactivation campaigns: maintenance reminders for lapsed maintenance agreement holders, seasonal tune-up offers, or equipment upgrade proposals for aging systems. Tracks reactivation rate and revenue recovered.

2-4 hrs/week saved Easy
ServiceTitan integration: Customer database and Equipment records. Requires a connected email/marketing platform for campaign delivery.
08
Warranty Tracking

Monitors equipment and labor warranties across your entire installed base. Auto-flags warranties expiring in 30, 60, and 90 days. Triggers proactive outreach to customers before expiration with renewal and extended warranty options. Prevents costly warranty claim denials from missed filing deadlines and creates upsell opportunities for maintenance plans.

2-3 hrs/week saved Medium
ServiceTitan integration: Installed Equipment API and warranty date fields. Requires consistent equipment installation data entry by field techs.
09
Inventory Alerts

Monitors truck stock and warehouse inventory against configurable reorder points. Auto-generates purchase orders when items hit minimum thresholds. Tracks usage patterns over time to optimize stock levels, reduce carrying costs, and prevent emergency supply runs that kill productivity. Alerts dispatchers when a tech's truck is missing parts needed for the next job.

3-5 hrs/week saved Medium
ServiceTitan integration: Inventory and Truck Replenishment APIs. Connects to supplier ordering portals for automated PO submission.
10
Review Generation

Automatically requests reviews after completed jobs where the customer gave a 5-star satisfaction score. Sends a timed follow-up (typically 2 hours post-completion) with direct links to Google Business Profile and relevant industry platforms. Monitors response rates and adjusts send timing for highest conversion. Flags negative feedback for immediate internal follow-up before it goes public.

1-3 hrs/week saved Easy
ServiceTitan integration: Job Completion webhooks and Customer Satisfaction data. Requires connected review platform APIs (Google, Yelp, etc.).

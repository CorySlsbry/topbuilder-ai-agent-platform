## 5. The differentiator — self-learning, on a leash (NEW section, prominent)
Headline: **ServiceTitan's agents are frozen. Yours keep learning.**
Sub: Most "AI tools" give you the same output on day 365 as day 1. Top Builder AI learns from what you
**approve and edit** — so it sounds more like your shop every month. The catch most vendors skip: it
learns *safely.* The governed 5-step loop:
1. **It proposes** — every recommendation comes with its reasoning, never a black box.
2. **You approve or edit** — your edits are the training signal; 3 matching corrections become a candidate rule.
3. **The eval gate** — a candidate must pass a safety check + a regression test before it can go live.
4. **You approve the rule** — nothing changes behavior until a human signs off. An approval queue, not autopilot.
5. **Canary rollout** — new rules roll out gradually, monitored for drift, auto-rolled-back if they slip.
Firewall callout (🔒): **The numbers are locked. Only the judgment improves.** Every dollar and hour
comes from deterministic, tested code — the learning layer can *never* touch a figure. The math stays
exact and auditable; only the wording and judgment get better. Your data never trains another shop's agents.

ServiceTitan's own help docs say Pricebook Pro's content is generic and refreshed monthly by ServiceTitan, and Pricebook Connect surfaces vendor cost changes for you to apply by hand, one material at a time. Neither one recomputes every affected flat-rate price across your book when a vendor cost moves, or shows you the margin dollar impact before you approve it.

Deterministic, tested code computes every figure — the AI only explains it, and nothing writes back without your approval.

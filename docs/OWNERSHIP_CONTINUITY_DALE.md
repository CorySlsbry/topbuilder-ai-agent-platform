# Continuity & Your Exit — Top Builder AI

*Prepared for Sam (Summit Heat Pumps). How you stay in control of your back office — and can walk away
any day without starting over — without anyone selling or handing over the platform code.*

---

## The short version

You should never have to "own the software" to be safe. What you actually need is the guarantee that **your
data, your setup, and what the system learns about your shop are yours — and that if you ever leave, you keep
operating from where you left off instead of going back to a blank spreadsheet.** You get all of that in
writing. The platform code itself stays ours — that's what lets us keep improving it for you every week and
keep it secure for every contractor on it — but you are never locked in and never held hostage.

## What is YOURS — always, exportable any day, no reason required

- **Your data.** And most of it never leaves your control in the first place — we *read* your ServiceTitan and
  QuickBooks; we don't replace them. Those stay your systems of record.
- **Your configuration** — your pricebook, your supplier mappings, your dispatch rules, your agent settings.
- **Your agents' learned playbook.** This is the important one: your agents don't learn as a black box we can
  hold hostage — they learn as a **plain-English rulebook** ("on collections, lead with the dollar exposure,
  then the single biggest action") that you can read, and that any competent person or system could pick up
  and run.

You get a complete export of all three, in standard open formats, **refreshed on a schedule, starting the day
you sign**, plus a written exit runbook. So if you ever fire us, you don't start from scratch — you start from
a full, current, readable copy of your entire operation.

## Your graceful exit — three outs, none of them a code sale

If you ever decide to leave, you don't lose what you built. We give you a real off-ramp so the day after you
cancel still runs:

| | What you get | The honest catch |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Database migration** | We hand your data off into a **database you control** (standard Postgres / open formats) and walk your team or your next vendor through the export runbook, so nothing has to be re-keyed. | You (or your next system) take over hosting and upkeep of that data. |
| **2. Bring-your-own keys** | The connections and the AI run on **your own ServiceTitan, QuickBooks, and AI API keys** — under your accounts, not ours — so the operational layer was never dependent on us in the first place, and stays entirely with you when you go. | You carry your own API/usage costs (you already would as an operator). |
| **3. Frozen local copy (non-learning parachute)** | If you cancel, we can stand up a **frozen, local snapshot** of your configured back office — same agents, same rules, your data — that keeps running from where it left off, on your own machine/server, on your own keys. You never drop back to the spreadsheet. | It's a **snapshot, not the live product**: it stops learning, gets no updates or new agents, and runs as **our licensed software** — you operate it, you don't own or receive the source. A parachute, not a purchase. |

**This is real, not theory:** the platform already installs with one command, takes your own
ServiceTitan/QuickBooks/AI keys, and runs on your own database — so the frozen local parachute is something we
can actually deliver, not a promise on paper.

## How the relationship is structured

The monthly is a **license to operate your instance** — not access we can yank, and not a rental. While you're
with us you get the live, always-improving product: weekly learning, new agents, security patches, support. If
you leave, outs 1–3 above are how you land softly. There is **no "buy the code" tier** — we don't sell or
transfer the platform, to you or anyone, because keeping it ours is what lets us keep building it and keep
every tenant's data walled off and secure.

## Two honest things (so there are no surprises)

1. **A frozen copy stops improving.** The weekly learning and the new agents only keep coming while we run it.
   The parachute keeps you operating — it does not keep you current. That's the honest reason staying on the
   live license almost always wins: you keep the upside without maintaining anything.
2. **Running it yourself isn't free.** The moment you take the database, the keys, or the frozen copy in-house,
   *you* inherit the hosting, the API/usage, and the upkeep. For most shops that's more cost and hassle than the
   subscription — which is exactly why the off-ramp is insurance, not the destination.

## The part that protects everyone

The platform code stays ours — it's what we run for many contractors, and your data is walled off from every
other client's at the database level (independently security-audited). What's yours is unambiguous and yours
forever: **your data, your configuration, and your learned rulebook.** The exact terms — the license scope, the
export and migration runbook, the frozen-copy license — get papered by a lawyer (BC-aware, since you're on the
Island). This sheet is the structure to instruct counsel with, not the final contract.

*Bottom line: you carry zero hostage risk, your trained back office walks out with you if you ever leave —
your data, your keys, even a working frozen copy — and you keep the upside of a system that keeps getting
smarter, without ever needing to buy or maintain the software.*

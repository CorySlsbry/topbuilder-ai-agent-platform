Pick the icon system

Every style shows the same six glyphs — crane, hard hat, wrench (construction) and ledger, dollar, chart (finance) — at marketing size, then at 19px inside a real app sidebar. That second row is the whole test: several of these look great big and turn to mush in the product. All hand-built SVG, so one set ships on the site and inside the app with no license, no attribution, and full recolouring. Reply with a number.

01Does Top Builder AI replace ServiceTitan?

No. It makes ServiceTitan more valuable. Top Builder AI plugs into the ServiceTitan you already run, never touches your front office, and runs the back-office work it leaves to QuickBooks: Money, Materials, People, and Paperwork. Nothing gets ripped out, your team keeps the scheduling board and CRM they already know, and your data is not moved. For scale: across the trades, ServiceTitan processed $62.0 billion of gross transaction volume in the 12 months ended July 31, 2024, up 23% year over year (ServiceTitan, Inc. Form S-1, SEC filing (2024)), and as of January 31, 2024 it served roughly 8,000 active customers, each with over $10,000 in annualized billings (ServiceTitan, Inc. Form S-1, SEC filing (2024)). Top Builder AI is built to sit on top of that platform, not to replace it.

02ServiceTitan already has AI agents. How is this different?

ServiceTitan's agents are good and getting better, especially Atlas, their new agentic AI. We differ in three honest ways. Ours learn from your shop's own approvals and corrections, while Titan Intelligence learns from aggregate data across every contractor. Ours stay advisory by default, you approve before anything happens, while Atlas takes action on its own. And we run on your actual QuickBooks books, not on ServiceTitan's data. Then we add the back-office work ServiceTitan leaves manual or to QuickBooks: automated Collections, Financial reconciled to your ledger, Inventory, Workforce, Documents, and Pricebook re-costing.

03How is Top Builder AI different from ServiceTitan Max?

ServiceTitan Max is front-office AI: booking, dispatch, field work, dynamic pricing, and the Atlas agent. ServiceTitan's broader platform adds job costing and bill capture, but those run on its own data with your books in QuickBooks. We do not compete with it there. We run the back-office work it leaves manual or leaves to QuickBooks. Its A/R follow-up is manual, so our Collections agent works the aging list 24/7 and drafts the dunning. We reconcile to your actual QuickBooks general ledger, and where ServiceTitan's AI optimizes on your metrics, our agents learn from the calls you approve and the edits you make. We plug in alongside it and never touch your system of record. Manual A/R is expensive across the trades: 82% of contractors now report payment delays of over 30 days, up from 49% just two years earlier (Rabbet 2024 Construction Payments Report (2024)).

04Will the AI change my numbers or hallucinate a figure?

It can't. Every dollar and hour is computed by deterministic, tested code. The AI only narrates and advises, and the learning layer is structurally blocked from touching a figure. The math stays exact, auditable, and isolated to your account. Only the wording and the judgment improve over time.

05Can the agents take actions on their own?

Only if you let them. Every agent ships advisory by default: it proposes, you approve. You can set each one to Off, Approve-first, or Auto, per agent, with full undo and an audit trail on every action. You see value in advisory mode before anything writes back to your systems.

06Is my data separate from other contractors?

Yes. Every tenant is isolated at the database level with row-level security, credentials are encrypted, and your data never trains another shop's agents. A rule your agents learn for your account stays in your account, never leaves it, and never benefits another contractor.

07What do the eight agents actually do?

Two run your front line alongside ServiceTitan's own agents: Booking 24/7 qualifies, slots, and values every inbound call, and Routing and Dispatch optimizes assignments by drive time, margin, parts, and overtime. Six run the back-office work ServiceTitan leaves manual or to QuickBooks: Financial projects cash and flags margin leakage, Inventory catches unbilled materials and prorates partial consumables, Workforce forecasts avoidable overtime and capacity, Documents files every inbound doc, Collections works the A/R aging worklist 24/7 and drafts the dunning, and Pricebook keeps materials costs current and re-prices every item under the sold-hour model. Money, Materials, People, Paperwork, A/R, Pricebook. The A/R work is the largest single drain across the industry: slow payments cost the U.S. construction industry an estimated $280 billion in 2024 (Rabbet 2024 Construction Payments Report (2024)).

08How does the self-learning work?

The agents learn from what you approve and what you edit. Three matching corrections become a candidate rule. The candidate must pass a safety check and a regression test, then you approve it, and it rolls out gradually with monitoring and rollback. Nothing changes behavior until a human signs off, and the learning layer can never touch a number. AI in the small-business back office is no longer early: 68% of small businesses now report using AI regularly, up from 48% in July 2024 (Intuit QuickBooks Small Business Insights survey (2025)).

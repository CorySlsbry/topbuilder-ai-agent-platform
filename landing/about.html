Trust and safety, in plain terms

Why the numbers can be trusted, and how the agents stay in their lane.

Authority only matters if it is paired with restraint. Here is how the platform is built so an AI agent can help with the back office without ever putting your books at risk.

Deterministic math, narrating AI

Every dollar and hour is computed by plain, tested code, not by a language model. The AI only narrates and advises. It cannot invent or change a figure, so the math stays exact, auditable, and the same every time you run it.

Advisory by default, you approve

Every agent ships in advisory mode: it proposes, you approve. You can set each one to Off, Approve-first, or Auto, per agent, with full undo and an audit trail on every action. Nothing writes back to your systems until you say so.

Your data, isolated

Every contractor is isolated at the database level with row-level security, credentials are encrypted, and your data never trains another shop's agents. A rule your agents learn stays in your account and never leaves it.

Learning that can never touch a figure

The agents learn from what you approve and what you edit, but only on the wording and judgment side. Three matching corrections become a candidate rule that must clear a safety check and a regression test, then a human approves it before it rolls out gradually with rollback.

Sits on top, never rips out

ServiceTitan, Buildertrend, Procore, and QuickBooks stay your systems of record. Top Builder AI plugs in alongside them and reads your jobs, calls, parts, and paperwork. Nothing is migrated, and if you ever leave, your business keeps running exactly as it did.

You own your data and stay portable

Your data, your configuration, and your agents' learned playbook are yours and exportable any day in standard formats. We can hand off to a database you own or run on your own keys. You are never held hostage, and you are never locked in.

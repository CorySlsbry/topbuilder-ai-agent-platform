What exactly is the A/R Collections Sprint?

A one-time, $97 done-for-you collections plan. What do I need to send you?

Just your A/R aging report: the standard export from QuickBooks (Reports, then Accounts Receivable Aging) or from ServiceTitan. PDF, CSV, or Excel all work. That single report is the entire data ask. The 24-hour clock starts the moment it lands in our inbox.

How is the queue ranked? Is an AI guessing?

The ranking is computed by deterministic, tested code, the same Collections agent engine inside the Top Builder AI platform: correct due-date aging, balance, age, and collectability signals, worst-first. The AI personalizes the wording of the emails and scripts; it is structurally blocked from computing or altering a figure. Every number traces back to your own aging report.

Will the emails and scripts damage my customer relationships?

They are written to do the opposite. Each one is firm on the money and respectful of the relationship, because most overdue customers are future repeat customers. The scripts include quiet-hours awareness and a professional tone ladder: the first touch is a friendly nudge, and the pressure only rises with age and silence. You review everything before it goes out; nothing is sent on your behalf.

How fast can I actually expect to collect?

Many overdue invoices are not disputes; they are drift: an invoice that fell through the cracks or a customer who pays whoever asks. Those often clear within days of the first correct touch. With the average contractor sitting on tens of thousands past 60 days, a single collected invoice typically covers the $97 a hundred times over. We do not promise a specific dollar amount, because your queue depends on your book, and honesty is the house style here.

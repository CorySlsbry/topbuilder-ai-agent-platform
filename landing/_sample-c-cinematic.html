Straight answers

Questions a serious operator asks.

Does this replace ServiceTitan? +

No, it makes ServiceTitan more valuable. We plug into it and run the back-office work it leaves to QuickBooks: live margin, materials, labor forecasting, and document filing. Keep every front-office workflow your team already knows.

ServiceTitan already has AI agents. How is this different? +

ServiceTitan's agents are good, especially Atlas, their new agentic AI. We differ honestly: ours learn from your shop's own approvals while theirs learns from aggregate trade data, ours stay advisory until you approve while Atlas acts on its own, and we run on your actual QuickBooks books while theirs runs on ServiceTitan's data. Then we add the back-office work it leaves manual or to QuickBooks: Financial reconciled to your ledger, Inventory, Workforce, Documents, automated Collections, and Pricebook re-costing.

Will the AI change my numbers or "hallucinate" a figure? +

It can't. Every dollar and hour is computed by deterministic, tested code. The AI only narrates and advises, the learning layer is structurally blocked from touching a figure. The math is exact and auditable; only the wording and judgment improve over time.

Can the agents take actions on their own? +

Only if you let them. Every agent ships advisory by default: it proposes, you approve. You can set each one to Off, Approve-first, or Auto, per agent, with full undo and an audit trail on every action.

Is my data safe and separate from other contractors? +

Yes. Every tenant is isolated at the database level (row-level security), credentials are encrypted, and your data never trains another shop's agents. A learned rule for your account stays in your account.

Do I own the code, and am I locked in? +

No, and you don't need to be. The platform stays ours, that's what keeps it improving every week and every shop's data isolated and secure. But your data, your configuration, and your agents' learned playbook are yours, exportable any day. If you ever leave, you keep operating instead of starting over: we migrate your data to a database you own, run it on your own API keys, or stand up a frozen local copy that keeps working from where it left off. Only the weekly learning and new agents stop. You're never held hostage.

What does the monthly cover, and why does it scale with my team? +

The monthly covers hosting, the compute the agents run on, ongoing model + safety updates, and the nightly learning that keeps your agents improving. It scales with the number of people actually using the system, so a 25-person shop (from $3,000/mo) never subsidizes a 250-person firm, and you only pay for the seats in use. It's billed off your connected field platform (ServiceTitan, Procore, or Buildertrend) and QuickBooks employee count, and your rate stays locked for as long as you stay.

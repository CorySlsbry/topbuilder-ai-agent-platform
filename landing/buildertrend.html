The questions, shallow to deep

Buildertrend and the back office, in plain answers.

01Does Buildertrend have AI?

Buildertrend has been adding AI-assisted features to its platform, and as of mid-2026 those features run on Buildertrend's own project, communication, and sales data. Check Buildertrend's current release notes for the specifics, because they are Buildertrend's to define. What stays open either way is the ledger question: your books live in QuickBooks, not in Buildertrend, and the follow-up, reconciliation, and payroll work that lives in those books is not what a project platform's AI is built to run.

That is the honest division of labor. Buildertrend is the system of record for the build, and any intelligence it ships makes the build side better. Top Builder AI is a back-office layer that runs on the money side, reconciled to your actual QuickBooks general ledger, and it installs alongside Buildertrend rather than competing with it.

02What does Buildertrend leave manual?

The work that lives in your books rather than in the project: chasing the invoice or draw that has not been paid, reconciling what Buildertrend says a job cost against what QuickBooks says actually posted, turning an emailed vendor bill into an A/P entry, verifying payroll before it runs, and tracking which certificates of insurance are about to expire. In most Buildertrend shops that work is done by one or two office people, by hand, when there is time.

  • Invoice and draw follow-up. The list of who owes what exists; a prioritized, ready-to-act follow-up queue does not.
  • QuickBooks reconciliation. Job records and the general ledger drift apart, and nobody notices until month-end or later.
  • A/P from vendor bills. Bills arrive by email and wait for a person to key them in.
  • Payroll verification. Overtime rules and claimed-versus-clocked gaps get checked manually, if at all.
  • Paperwork. Filing, COI expiry tracking, and signature chasing land on whoever is nearest.

03How do I automate invoice and draw follow-up for a Buildertrend business?

Connect an agent to your open receivables, reconcile them against QuickBooks first so you never chase money that already arrived, score each open item by age, balance, and customer history, and generate a ranked follow-up queue that a person approves before anything reaches a client. That is exactly what Top Builder AI's Collections agent does: it works the aging list around the clock, drafts the follow-up email, SMS, or call script for each item, and tracks every promise-to-pay, and every send requires a department lead's approval, always.

The reconciliation-first order matters more on builder work than anywhere else, because draws and progress invoices are exactly the payments that land in QuickBooks without being applied to the matching job record. The full mechanics, including the scoring model and a worked example, are in our guide to automating collections for a Buildertrend shop.

04How do I reconcile Buildertrend jobs to QuickBooks?

Treat QuickBooks as the financial source of truth and Buildertrend as the project source of truth, then compare them on a cycle: match jobs and customers across the two systems, compare billed, received, and committed amounts, and flag every place they disagree. Top Builder AI's Financial agent runs that loop and turns each mismatch into a worklist item, an unapplied payment for the bookkeeper, a bill that never posted, a change that exists in one system only, so the margin and cash figures you act on match the books you file.

One honest caveat: the QuickBooks connection is activated per customer during install and fails closed until then. If the accounting connection is not yet active, the agents say "not connected" rather than guess a number. Every figure, once connected, is computed by deterministic, tested code; the AI only explains it.

05How do I automate collections for a Buildertrend business?

Run the four-step loop: reconcile receivables against QuickBooks, score the genuine open items by age, balance, history, and margin, propose the specific next action for each one, and put a human approval in front of every send. Top Builder AI's Collections agent runs that loop on a configurable cadence and never contacts a client on its own. Given that 82% of contractors now face payment delays of more than 30 days, per the Rabbet 2024 Construction Payments Report, consistent follow-up is the difference between financing your clients interest-free and getting paid.

The step-by-step playbook, with the aging math and a worked example, is in How to Automate Collections for Your Buildertrend Shop.

07How is this different from Buildertrend's own features?

They cover different ground, and we say so plainly. Buildertrend's features, including whatever AI it ships, run on Buildertrend's own data and make the project side better: scheduling, client communication, selections, proposals. Top Builder AI runs the QuickBooks-ledger back office that sits outside any project platform's data: collections follow-up, reconciliation to your actual general ledger, A/P drafting from vendor bills, payroll checks, and document filing. You keep Buildertrend exactly as it is; we install alongside it and never touch your system of record.

What data do I need to provide?

A QuickBooks read-only invite (sent to support@topbuilderai.com) plus a ServiceTitan report export, or a recent weekly scorecard if you already keep one in a spreadsheet. It is the real, live Top Builder AI platform: your own isolated, tenant-secured account with your data in it. You log in whenever you want for 14 days. Every figure is computed by deterministic, tested code from your data; the AI writes the narration and is structurally blocked from touching a number.

Access ends, and that is all that happens. There is no auto-renewal and no card charged. If you want to keep it, you tell us and we talk about the full install, with your $27 credited. What is the seasonal forecast band?

Each KPI trend is drawn against a forecast band computed from seasonal patterns, so you can tell the difference between a number that is genuinely off track and one that is just doing what that month always does. It is the difference between panicking about a slow February and spotting a margin slide in your busiest month, which is the one that actually costs you.

Does the $27 really credit toward an install?

Yes, dollar for dollar, toward any Top Builder AI install. During quarters when the install fee is $0, the credit applies to your first month instead. The full install adds the eight self-learning agents that act on what the scorecard shows: collections, pricebook updates, booking, dispatch, and the rest, all advisory by default.

How is this different from the reports I already have in ServiceTitan?

ServiceTitan reports on ServiceTitan; your books live in QuickBooks; and neither puts the whole company on one page scored against targets. The Scorecard joins your field data and your actual books, scores every KPI against its target, and rolls it up to one executive view with trend lines and the seasonal band. It answers "how is the company doing" in one glance, which is a different job than running a report.

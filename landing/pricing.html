The pricing model, in one sentence

The install is $0 this quarter (normally $8,000), or you can pay the install and keep the monthly flexible.

No per-feature add-ons and no per-seat surprise math. Every tier includes all eight self-learning agents, every update, and the nightly learning. Your rate is locked for as long as you stay, and a 30-day board-ready-or-free guarantee covers it.

Pricing questions

Straight answers on what it costs.

How much does Top Builder AI cost? +

It is a one-time install plus a monthly that scales with how many people use the system. The monthly is $3,000/mo for up to 25 people, $4,500/mo for 26 to 75, $6,000/mo for 76 to 125, $8,000/mo for 126 to 250, and custom above 250. The install is $0 this quarter (normally $8,000), or you can pay the install and keep the monthly flexible.

Why does the monthly scale with my team size? +

Because a 25-person shop should not pay what a 250-person firm pays. The monthly is billed off your connected field platform (ServiceTitan, Procore, or Buildertrend) and QuickBooks employee count, so you only pay for the people actually using the system. It scales smoothly as you grow, always at the rate you started on, which stays locked for as long as you stay.

Is the $0 install really free, and what is the catch? +

Yes. The one-time install (normally $8,000) is $0 for contractors who join this quarter's onboarding cohort. There is no catch: you start your monthly, and your rate is locked for as long as you stay. The only limit is capacity, since every install is done hands-on by the developer, so a small number of contractors are onboarded each quarter.

Can I pay the install instead of locking a founding rate? +

Yes. If you would rather pay the one-time install ($8,000) and keep your monthly flexible, you can. The $0-install offer trades the waived install for a locked founding rate; paying the install keeps your monthly arrangement more flexible. Both paths include all eight agents and the same board-ready-or-free guarantee.

What does the monthly include? +

Hosting, the compute the agents run on, ongoing model and safety updates, and the nightly learning that keeps your agents improving. Every agent and every update is included, with no per-feature add-ons. You are never upsold a feature you already expected to be there.

Is there a contract or a setup fee beyond the install? +

There is one install (waived this quarter) and one monthly, nothing else. There is no second install fee when you move up a tier, and no per-feature add-ons. You can upgrade or downgrade as your team size changes, and the monthly simply follows your connected employee count.

What is the board-ready-or-free guarantee? +

If your first month of reports is not something you would hand to a partner or a lender, your install fee is refunded in full and you keep everything the agents produced that month. On the $0-install offer there is no install fee to refund, so we waive your first month's monthly instead. The risk is on us.

Why do you onboard in quarterly cohorts? +

Every install is done hands-on by the developer, not self-serve, so we bring on a limited number of contractors each quarter. That is real capacity, not a fake countdown.
